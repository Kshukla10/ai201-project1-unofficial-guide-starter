Topic: Student Centers and Dining Locations

The UIC Student Centers provide spaces and services that support student life and campus activities. Since opening in 1969, the Student Centers have served as a central hub for the UIC community.

Student Center East (SCE) offers a variety of food options, meeting spaces, and student services. Additional amenities include the University Bookstore and recreational facilities. SCE serves thousands of students, faculty, staff, and visitors throughout the academic year.

Student Center West (SCW) provides similar services on a smaller scale and is designed to meet the needs of students on the west campus, particularly health sciences and medical students. Food services, lounge areas, and meeting spaces are major features of the building.

According to the Student Centers mission statement, the centers serve as the heart of UIC by supporting student engagement, campus life, community building, convenient services, and high-quality facilities.
