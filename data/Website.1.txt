
UIC Dining Services Overview

UIC Dining Services provides a variety of dining options for students, faculty, and staff across campus.

Dining Services Include:
- Dining Halls
- Retail Dining Locations
- Meal Plans
- Catering Services
- Nutrition Resources


Meal Plans:

Students can purchase meal plans that grant access to campus dining halls and other dining services. 

Both residential and commuter meal plans are available. A "meal swipe" refers to the swipes you use to enter the dining halls. Each meal swipe allows entry into any of our dining halls, where you can enjoy a variety of meals. All meal swipes are ready to use with your I-Card.

If you are on a meal swipes-per-week plan, your number of weekly swipes will reset every Monday morning. Blaze plan users can use their swipes throughout the semester. In addition to using swipes at the dining halls, residential meal plans with meal exchanges let you use a select number of swipes on designated menu items at participating retail locations.


 Flames Fare:

Flames Fare is dining dollars or tax-free money loaded onto your I-Card that students, faculty, and staff can use at participating retail locations on campus. Flames Fare balances carry over from the fall to spring semester and from spring to summer session. Please note that Flames Fare expires at the end of Summer Session II; all unused Flames Fare will be forfeited.

You can view all locations that accept Flames Fare [here](https://uicdining.sodexomyway.com/en-us/locations/).

Guest Passes:

All residential meal plans that offer guest passes include a set number each semester. These guest passes allow students to treat friends, family, or visitors to a meal at either 605 Commons Dining Hall or JST Dining Hall. Guest passes do not roll over and must be used within the semester. Students may choose to donate guest passes to the "U and I Care" meal swipe program, which supports students facing food insecurity.

Residential meal plans are required for students living in the: 
- Academic & Residential Complex (ARC)
- Courtyard
- Commons North, West, South
- James Stukel Towers (JST)

We welcome residents in Marie Robinson Hall, Thomas Beckham Hall, the Single Student Residence, and Polk Street Residence, as well as commuter students, faculty, and staff to participate in campus dining with a Blaze Meal Plan.


Dining Locations:

Major dining locations include:
- 605 Commons Dining Hall in Student Center East
- JST Café in James Stukel Towers

Additional retail dining options are available throughout campus.



Menus and Nutrition:

Students can view menus, nutrition information, dining locations, and operating hours through the Everyday App. The Everyday App makes life easier and helps you stay on track with your nutritional needs by allowing you to:
- View retail and residential dining menus
- Save time when deciding what to order in line
- See campus events
- Avoid allergens by setting dietary preferences
- View healthy recipes, tips, and tricks
- Manage subscriptions and memberships


 Reusable Container Program:

UIC Dining offers the Flames Go Green Reusable Container Program to promote sustainability and reduce waste. Our containers are available at both dining halls (605 Commons and JST Café).

How it Works:
- Buy a Flames Go Green reusable container for a one-time fee.
- Available at front registers when entering 605 Commons or JST Café.
- Cost: $8 for the container or $11 for the container and cup combo.
