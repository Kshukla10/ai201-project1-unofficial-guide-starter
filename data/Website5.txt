Topic: UIC Campus Dining Services

UIC Dining Services provides dining options for students, faculty, staff, and visitors across the University of Illinois Chicago campus. Dining services include dining halls, retail dining locations, meal plans, catering services, nutrition resources, and sustainability initiatives.

Dining Locations:
Campus dining locations include Student Center East, Student Center West, James Stukel Towers, the Behavioral Science Building, University Hall, the Daley Library, the Academic and Residential Complex, and other campus facilities. Dining options include dining halls, cafes, convenience markets, and national restaurant brands.

Meal Plans:
Students can purchase meal plans that provide access to dining halls and other campus dining services. Meal plans include meal swipes, meal exchanges, and Flames Fare, which can be used at participating campus dining locations. Frequently asked questions and meal plan resources are available through UIC Dining Services.

Dining Halls:
Major dining halls include 605 Commons in Student Center East and JST Café in James Stukel Towers. These dining halls offer a variety of food stations, including grill items, pizza, pasta, salad bars, deli options, and rotating specials. 605 Commons also offers Simply 3, a station designed for guests avoiding common allergens such as peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, and gluten.

Nutrition and Sustainability:
UIC Dining provides nutrition information, menu resources, and sustainability programs. Initiatives include the Flames Go Green reusable container program and the U&I Care Round Up Initiative, which supports students facing financial insecurity through voluntary donations at checkout.

Additional Services:
UIC Dining supports catering, food trucks, Grubhub ordering, nutrition education, special dietary accommodations, and campus dining events. Students can also access menus, nutrition information, and dining hours through dining resources and mobile applications.
